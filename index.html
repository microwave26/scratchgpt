What it does

Small project, big brain energy

A simple way to bring an AI chat experience into a block-based project.

💬

Chat with AI

Send a question and receive an AI-generated response through the ScratchGPT backend.

🧠

Conversation history

Use the project’s history feature to view the conversation for the current session.

🧩

Made for TurboWarp

Uses a custom extension, so it needs TurboWarp rather than the standard Scratch editor.
